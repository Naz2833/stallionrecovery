Cookie Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Stallion Recovery 24/7 uses cookies and similar technologies on this website, in line with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and UK GDPR.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the website owner.

2. Cookies We Use

This website is intentionally kept simple and currently uses only strictly necessary cookies:

  • Cookie consent preference — stores whether you have accepted or declined cookies on this site, so we don't ask you again on every visit. This is stored using your browser's local storage rather than a traditional cookie, but is covered here for transparency.

We do not currently use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies. If this changes in future (for example, if we add website analytics), this policy and our cookie consent banner will be updated accordingly, and consent will be requested before any non-essential cookies are set.

3. Managing Cookies

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies using the banner shown when you first visit this site. You can also control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how this website functions.

4. More Information

For details on how we handle personal data more generally, please see our Privacy Policy. If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy, contact us at Stallion247recovery@gmail.com.
