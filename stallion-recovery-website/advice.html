Stay Safe

If You Break Down, Follow These Steps

  1. Get to safety first

    Put your hazard lights on. If you can safely do so, move off the carriageway — onto the hard shoulder, a layby or a side road. If you're on a motorway and can't get off it, stay in the vehicle with your seatbelt on unless it's unsafe to do so, or exit on the passenger side away from traffic and stand behind a barrier if there is one.

  2. Call for help

    If you or anyone else is in immediate danger, call 999 first. Otherwise, call or text us and we'll get moving straight away.

  3. Know your location

    Have your postcode, nearest landmark, or motorway junction/mile marker ready. If you're not sure, apps like what3words can help pinpoint your exact position quickly.

  4. Stay safe while you wait

    Keep away from moving traffic, stay behind any barriers if you can, and keep your hazard lights on. Wear a hi-vis vest if you have one.

  5. Have your details ready

    When we arrive, it helps to have your registration, make and model, and a quick description of the fault ready to go — it speeds things up on both ends.

How Much Does Vehicle Recovery Cost?

There's no single flat rate for vehicle recovery — the price depends on a few things:

  • Distance — how far we need to travel to reach you, and how far the vehicle needs to be taken.
  • Vehicle type — cars, vans and larger vehicles can require different equipment.
  • Time of day — call-outs late at night or on holidays can differ from daytime jobs.
  • Accessibility — an easy roadside pickup is quicker than a tricky recovery from a ditch, verge or tight space.

The best way to get an accurate price is to call or text us with the details — we'll give you a clear, upfront quote before we set off, so there are no surprises.
